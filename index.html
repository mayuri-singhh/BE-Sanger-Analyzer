🧬 Base-edit Sanger Seq Analyzer

Paste your CDS and guides (sense or antisense), add .abi reads, and get the % of every base at each protospacer position plus the codon and amino-acid change. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded, and refreshing resets everything.

1CDS and guides
2Sequencing reads
    Optional: unedited control
      1Upload a trace from the unedited (WT) sample, sequenced with the same primer.
      2Its non-reference signal at each protospacer position becomes that position's baseline.
      3The baseline is subtracted from every edited read (% changed = sample − control), and calls are tested against zero noise.
      4Your files are never modified; without a control, background is estimated from flanking bases only.
      3Settings
      Semi-quantitative: Sanger has a ≈5% detection limit. Edited = % changed ≥ threshold and p<0.01 vs. background noise. Confirm key alleles by NGS.
